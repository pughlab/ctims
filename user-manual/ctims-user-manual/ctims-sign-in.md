## Steps to Sign in:&#x20;

Step 1: Connect to UHN Network/VPN&#x20;

Step 2: Go to [https://ctims.ca](https://ctims.ca/) &#x20;

Step 3: Sign in using your existing UHN credentials (T-ID or email)&#x20;



{% hint style="warning" %}
Please note: CTIMS leverages the UHN Active Directory to authenticate users. If you are having any issues with sign in, you may need to reset your UHN credentials. Please contact HelpDesk for assistance. &#x20;
{% endhint %}
